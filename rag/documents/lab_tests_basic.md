## Hemoglobin
aliases: Hemoglobin, Haemoglobin, Hb, Hgb
Hemoglobin is a protein in red blood cells that carries oxygen around the body. Values outside the reference range can have several different causes, which only a doctor can work out after looking at the full picture.

## Red blood cell count
aliases: RBC, Red Blood Cells, RBC Count, Erythrocytes
This test counts the red blood cells, the cells that carry oxygen. It is usually read together with hemoglobin and related red cell indices.

## Hematocrit
aliases: Hematocrit, Haematocrit, PCV, Packed Cell Volume, HCT
Hematocrit (PCV) shows what share of the blood is made up of red blood cells. It is read together with hemoglobin and the red blood cell count.

## MCV, MCH and MCHC
aliases: MCV, MCH, MCHC, RDW, red cell indices
These are red cell indices. They describe the average size of red blood cells, how much hemoglobin each carries, and how much the sizes vary. Doctors use them together with hemoglobin to understand blood test patterns.

## White blood cell count
aliases: WBC, TLC, Total Leucocyte Count, Total WBC Count, Leukocytes, White Blood Cells
White blood cells are part of the body's defence system. This test counts them. Values outside the reference range are not specific to any single cause, so a doctor reads them with the rest of the report and the symptoms.

## Differential count
aliases: Neutrophils, Lymphocytes, Monocytes, Eosinophils, Basophils, Differential Leucocyte Count, DLC
The differential count shows the share of each type of white blood cell. Each type has a different job in the body's defence system. A doctor reads these together with the total white cell count.

## Platelet count
aliases: Platelets, Platelet Count, PLT, Thrombocytes
Platelets are small blood cells that help the blood to clot. This test counts them. The meaning of a result outside the reference range depends on the full clinical picture.

## ESR
aliases: ESR, Erythrocyte Sedimentation Rate
ESR measures how fast red blood cells settle in a tube of blood. It is a general test that is not specific to one condition, so it is always interpreted by a doctor along with other findings.

## Fasting blood glucose
aliases: Glucose, Blood Sugar, Fasting Glucose, FBS, Glucose Fasting, Glucose Random, Glucose PP, Post Prandial Glucose
This test measures the amount of sugar (glucose) in the blood. Reference ranges depend on whether the sample was taken fasting or after a meal, so the range printed in the report for that sample type is the one to use.

## HbA1c
aliases: HbA1c, Glycosylated Hemoglobin, Glycated Hemoglobin, A1c
HbA1c reflects the average blood sugar level over the previous few months. It is interpreted by a doctor together with other tests and symptoms.

## Total cholesterol
aliases: Cholesterol, Total Cholesterol
Cholesterol is a fatty substance in the blood. The total cholesterol result is read together with the other lipid tests such as LDL, HDL and triglycerides.

## LDL cholesterol
aliases: LDL, LDL Cholesterol, Low Density Lipoprotein, VLDL
LDL and VLDL are two of the types of cholesterol carriers in the blood. Doctors look at them together with the full lipid profile and each person's own situation.

## HDL cholesterol
aliases: HDL, HDL Cholesterol, High Density Lipoprotein
HDL is one of the types of cholesterol carriers in the blood. It is read together with the full lipid profile.

## Triglycerides
aliases: Triglycerides, TG
Triglycerides are a type of fat found in the blood. The result can be affected by whether the person had eaten before the sample was taken, so it is read with the full lipid profile.

## Creatinine
aliases: Creatinine, Serum Creatinine, eGFR
Creatinine is a waste product that the kidneys filter out of the blood. It is used by doctors as one of the measures of how the kidneys are working, always together with other information.

## Urea and BUN
aliases: Urea, Blood Urea, BUN, Blood Urea Nitrogen
Urea is a waste product that the kidneys remove from the blood. It is read together with creatinine and other kidney tests.

## Uric acid
aliases: Uric Acid, Serum Uric Acid
Uric acid is a waste product found in the blood. A doctor interprets this value together with symptoms and other tests.

## Liver enzymes
aliases: ALT, SGPT, AST, SGOT, ALP, Alkaline Phosphatase, GGT
ALT (SGPT), AST (SGOT), ALP and GGT are enzymes found in the liver and some other organs. Doctors read them together as part of a liver panel; one value alone does not explain a cause.

## Bilirubin
aliases: Bilirubin, Total Bilirubin, Direct Bilirubin, Indirect Bilirubin
Bilirubin is a yellow substance made when old red blood cells are broken down. It is processed by the liver. It is read with the other liver tests.

## Albumin and proteins
aliases: Albumin, Total Protein, Globulin, A/G Ratio
Albumin and globulin are proteins in the blood. They are measured as part of liver and general health panels and are read together.

## TSH
aliases: TSH, Thyroid Stimulating Hormone
TSH is a hormone made by the pituitary gland in the brain that tells the thyroid gland how much hormone to make. It is usually read together with T3 and T4.

## T3 and T4
aliases: T3, T4, Free T3, Free T4, FT3, FT4, Thyroxine, Triiodothyronine
T3 and T4 are hormones made by the thyroid gland. They are read together with TSH.

## Vitamin D
aliases: Vitamin D, 25-OH Vitamin D, 25 Hydroxy Vitamin D
This test measures the level of vitamin D in the blood. Whether any action is needed is a decision for the doctor.

## Vitamin B12
aliases: Vitamin B12, B12, Cobalamin
This test measures the level of vitamin B12 in the blood. Whether any action is needed is a decision for the doctor.

## Ferritin and iron
aliases: Ferritin, Serum Iron, Iron, TIBC, Transferrin Saturation
Ferritin and iron tests show how much iron the body has in store and in the blood. They are read together with hemoglobin and other blood tests.

## Sodium and potassium
aliases: Sodium, Potassium, Na, K, Chloride, Electrolytes
Sodium, potassium and chloride are electrolytes, minerals in the blood that help nerves and muscles work and keep the body's fluid balance. Results outside the reference range should be reviewed by a doctor.

## Calcium
aliases: Calcium, Serum Calcium
Calcium is a mineral in the blood that is important for bones, muscles and nerves. It is read with other tests by a doctor.

## C-reactive protein
aliases: CRP, C-Reactive Protein, hs-CRP
CRP is a protein that the body makes in response to inflammation. It is a general marker and not specific to one condition, so a doctor interprets it with the rest of the findings.
