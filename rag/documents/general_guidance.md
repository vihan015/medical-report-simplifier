## What a reference range means
aliases: reference range, normal range, reference interval, biological reference interval
A reference range is the set of values found in most healthy people, as defined by the laboratory that ran the test. Different laboratories and methods can use slightly different ranges, so the range printed on your own report is the one to compare with. A value outside the range does not by itself mean a disease, and a value inside the range does not rule one out.

## Why a doctor must review the report
aliases: doctor review, medical advice, interpretation
Lab results are interpreted together with symptoms, medical history, medicines and other tests. Only a qualified doctor can do this. This tool only explains what the printed values and ranges say in simple words.
